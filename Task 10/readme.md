## Dashboard Summary

The Tableau dashboard shows business performance across **departments, cities, gender, and employee data** using multiple visualizations.

* **Heat Map:** The **IT and Sales departments** show the highest Monthly Sales K values across most cities. Sales reaches the highest value of **13,132K in Pune**, while IT also performs strongly across all cities.
* **Tree Map:** **Jaipur** has the highest Annual Salary value at approximately **323.37M**, followed by **Chennai (319.21M)** and **Mumbai (279.91M)**.
* **Horizontal Bar Chart:** The **Male and Female categories** have much higher values than the **Other** category, with Male slightly higher than Female.
* **Bubble Chart:** The bubble chart shows the distribution of employees using **Employee ID**, with bubble sizes representing differences in the selected measure.
* **Overall Pattern:** IT and Sales are the strongest departments in terms of monthly sales, while Finance and HR generally show lower sales values.

5 Business Insights

1. **Sales and IT are the strongest-performing departments** based on Monthly Sales K.
2. **Pune has the highest Sales department value at 13,132K.**
3. IT maintains consistently high sales across different cities.
4. **Jaipur and Chennai have the highest total annual salary values** in the Treemap.
5. The dashboard shows noticeable differences in performance between departments and cities, indicating that location and department both influence business performance.

 2 Recommendations

1. **Focus more resources on high-performing IT and Sales teams** and study their successful practices.
2. **Investigate lower-performing department-city combinations** and introduce targeted training, sales strategies, and resource allocation to improve their performance.
